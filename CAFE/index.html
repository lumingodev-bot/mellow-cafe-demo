Made slowly, served warmly

Mellow started with a simple idea: a place where nobody rushes you. We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise and keep the kettle on for whoever walks in.

Bring a book, a laptop or a friend. There's free Wi-Fi, comfy seating and a quiet corner for meetings.
